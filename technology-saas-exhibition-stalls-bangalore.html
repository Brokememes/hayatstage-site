Built For Technology & SaaS

Exhibition stalls engineered for Technology & SaaS exhibitors.

Technology and software exhibitors live or die on the demo. Hayat Stage builds booths designed around working screens, live product walkthroughs and clean, brand-accurate signage — plus the stages behind partner kickoffs, developer conferences and operations meetings.

What the demo actually needs

In practice, that starts well before the stall goes up. A working screen on the show floor needs planned power runs, not an extension cord routed around a reception desk — and a live product walkthrough needs a demo station positioned where a prospect can actually see the screen without craning around a pillar or a passing crowd. We plan power drops, structured cabling routes and sightlines to demo stations at the design stage, alongside the signage and graphics that carry your brand system — so the booth that gets rendered in the 3D concept is the one that still works when someone's mid-pitch on day two of the show.

Who this vertical covers

"Technology and SaaS" covers more ground in Bangalore than a software dashboard on a monitor. The city's exhibitor base runs from SaaS platforms showing a live product tour, through hardware and electronics brands with physical units on the counter, to security and surveillance technology, and RF, antenna and test-and-measurement equipment that needs a demo bay sturdy enough to hold real instrumentation — the kind of booth we've built for antenna and test-systems specialists and RF/microwave equipment brands on Bangalore show floors. Each of those needs a different floor plan, but the same discipline: the product is the reason someone stops, so the stall has to get out of its way.

Booths — and the stages behind them

Bangalore's tech calendar isn't only trade-show floors. The city hosts a dense concentration of SaaS headquarters, product engineering teams and global capability centres, and a large share of our tech-vertical work here is the stage behind a partner kickoff, a developer-tour stop or an internal ops meeting rather than a public exhibition stall — LED backdrops, branded entrance structures and full stage builds for the kind of on-campus or off-site event a Bangalore-based tech company runs several times a year. We treat both sides of that as one production discipline: the same crew that fabricates an exhibition stall for BIEC also builds the kickoff stage for the same brand's India partner event.

BIEC on Tumkur Road takes the large-format shows — international electronics and technology exhibitions where double-decker stands and hall compliance paperwork are part of the build, not an afterthought. KTPO in Whitefield sits inside Bangalore's tech corridor itself, and tends to suit mid-size and modular stalls for corporate and product-focused exhibitions where exhibitors are based five minutes from the venue rather than flying in. We build for both, and plan crew logistics around Bangalore's traffic patterns on show days so load-in doesn't eat into your setup window.

Planning Ahead

Planning Your Tech Stall In Bangalore.

Tech exhibitors tend to have more moving parts to plan for than a standard trade-show stand — a demo build, sometimes hardware in transit, and a brand system with more rules than a typical exhibitor's guidelines. Here's what to have ready before you reach out, and what to expect on timing.

Lead Time

Custom and double-decker stalls for BIEC generally need longer design and fabrication lead time than a modular KTPO stand — start the conversation as soon as your hall allocation is confirmed, and earlier still if your demo setup involves specialised or imported equipment that needs its own transit and installation planning.

What To Bring To The First Call

Your brand guidelines or design system file, a rough list of what's being demoed (screens, hardware units, or both) and their power and space needs, and your hall size and stall position if you have it. The more of this you have upfront, the faster we can turn around a concept.

Bangalore Logistics

BIEC and KTPO Whitefield sit on opposite sides of the city, so factor venue location into your setup-day schedule if your team is coordinating from a Bangalore office. We plan load-in and crew logistics around each venue's access rules and Bangalore's peak-hour traffic separately — they're not interchangeable.

FAQ

Technology & SaaS exhibition stalls in Bangalore, answered.

Can you integrate live product demos into a tech booth?

Yes — we plan power runs, networking and demo-station sightlines into the layout from the concept stage, so screens and live walkthroughs work exactly as intended once the show floor is open, not as an add-on layered in later.

Do you build stages for partner kickoffs and conferences, not just booths?

Yes — LED backdrops, truss and lighting rigs, and full stage builds for partner kickoffs, developer tours and ops meetings are a regular part of our Bangalore tech-vertical work, on top of exhibition stalls.

How much does a stall cost in Bangalore?

It depends on size, format, finish and demo complexity — a modular KTPO stand and a large-format BIEC double-decker sit at very different price points. Share your hall size and demo requirements and we'll send an itemised quote.

Which venues do you cover in Bangalore?

Any Bangalore venue, including BIEC on Tumkur Road for large-format shows and KTPO Whitefield for modular and mid-size corporate exhibitions.

How far in advance should we start planning?

As soon as your hall allocation is confirmed for a BIEC show. For KTPO or an off-floor event like a partner kickoff, a shorter runway can work — but earlier is always easier if your demo setup involves imported or specialised hardware.

Can you build for a partner event or product launch on a Bangalore tech campus, not just at BIEC or KTPO?

Yes — a large share of our Bangalore tech-vertical work is stage and event builds on company campuses or at off-site venues for kickoffs and developer events, run by the same team that builds our exhibition stalls.
