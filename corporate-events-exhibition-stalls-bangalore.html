Foundation days, award ceremonies and company-wide kickoffs need a different kind of build than a trade-show booth — large-format entrance structures, stages rated for thousands of attendees, and lighting and AV that carry a room. The difference shows up before the audience even sits down. A trade-show booth is built to be walked past and glanced into; an entrance structure for a foundation day or a partner summit has to be walked through — which changes the engineering. We plan for crowd flow at the arch, load-test the structure against wind and footfall inside a hall or on a lawn, and route cabling so the red carpet stays trip-free under a few hundred pairs of shoes. On stage, the same shift happens: a booth counter has to hold a laptop and a banner; a stage for an award night or a company kickoff has to carry a deck load rated for presenters, trophy tables and sometimes a live band, plus the truss overhead for moving-head lighting, line-array sound and an LED wall — all rigged, not just placed. For Bangalore's tech and SaaS floor plans in particular, that usually means designing around auditorium seating, screen sightlines from the back row, and a stage depth that can hold a panel discussion one hour and a product reveal the next.

This side of the business covers a wider band of events than the name "exhibition stalls" suggests. We've built entrance arches and red-carpet walks for foundation-day anniversaries, staged award ceremonies with trophy platforms and cued lighting, produced outdoor kickoff sets for all-hands town halls, and put together photo backdrop walls and interview sets for product and partner launches. Bangalore's calendar leans heavily on tech-sector milestones — annual conferences, developer-relations tours and campus town halls among them — and each format asks for a different stage geometry and AV plan even when the branding stays the same.

On a trade-show floor, a stall is usually one contractor's job. A corporate event pulls in a stage carpenter, a rigger, a lighting designer, a sound engineer and often a video or livestream operator — and when those are separate vendors, someone on your side ends up chasing all of them for a single load-in schedule. We run stage, AV and lighting as one production unit: one site walkthrough, one rigging plan, one crew on the ground for load-in and load-out, and one point of contact if something needs to change on show day. For a Bangalore venue like BIEC or a campus auditorium in Whitefield, that usually means one production manager coordinating the hall's structural sign-off with the AV rig, instead of three vendors working off three separate briefs.

Planning Ahead

Planning a Corporate Event in Bangalore.

Before you reach out, it helps to have a rough shape for three things: how many people are attending, what kind of event it is (a seated award night reads very differently from a standing town hall or a launch with a press wall), and a date or date range. We can start scoping a concept even before the venue is fully locked, but the venue does drive a lot of the engineering — a BIEC hall build has to work within the centre's structural and hall compliance requirements, while a KTPO or Whitefield venue is usually a lighter, modular build suited to a tech-company campus footprint.

Lead time matters most for anything with a large-format entrance structure or a multi-truss stage rig, since those need structural drawings and rigging plans signed off before load-in. The earlier you bring us in — ideally as soon as the date and venue are confirmed — the more room we have to design the entrance experience properly rather than compress it into a rushed build. Bangalore's Whitefield and Outer Ring Road corridors also see heavy weekday traffic, so load-in and load-out windows around campus venues are worth flagging early if your event falls on a working day.

If you already have a rough attendee count, a preferred format and a shortlist of venues, that's enough for us to put together a first concept and quote.

FAQ

Corporate Events exhibition stalls in Bangalore, answered.

Do you handle large-format corporate events with 1,000+ attendees in Bangalore?

Yes — foundation-day celebrations, award ceremonies and company-wide kickoffs in this scale range are a regular part of our Bangalore event work, with stages and rigging engineered for the audience size.

Can you build branded entrance structures and red-carpet arrivals for a foundation day or award night?

Yes — large-format entrance arches, signage walls and red-carpet walkways are a core part of our corporate event scope, not an add-on.

We're planning an event at a BIEC hall or a KTPO/Whitefield venue — can you work within the venue's structural rules?

Yes. BIEC builds are fabricated to the centre's hall compliance and structural requirements; KTPO and Whitefield venues are typically lighter modular builds suited to tech-campus floor plans, and we design to each venue's specific rules.

Do you run AV and lighting yourselves, or do we need to hire a separate production vendor?

We run stage, AV and lighting as one production unit — sound, lighting and LED walls are planned and rigged by the same team building the stage, so you're not coordinating multiple vendors' schedules.

How far in advance should a Bangalore tech company plan a stage and AV build for a conference-style or campus event?

As early as your date and venue are confirmed. Entrance structures and multi-truss stage rigs need structural sign-off before load-in, so the earlier we're looped in, the more design work — rather than rushed assembly — goes into the final build.

How much does corporate event production cost in Bangalore?

It depends on scale, format and finish — a seated award night, a standing town hall and a press-launch set all price differently. Share your attendee count, format and venue and we'll send an itemised quote.
