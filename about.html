About Hayat Stage

Built By Exhibitors, For Exhibitors.

Hayat Stage is an exhibition stall design and event management company built around one idea: exhibitors shouldn't have to manage five vendors to get one stall built.

Our Story

Hayat Stage was started to bring design discipline and on-time execution to exhibition stall building — an industry where delays and vendor handoffs routinely cost exhibitors their opening-day setup. Today we design, fabricate and install stalls for trade shows and corporate events across five major Indian cities.

Our Mission

Give every exhibitor — from first-time to enterprise — a stall that performs as well as it looks, delivered without drama.
