Built For Corporate Events

Exhibition stalls engineered for Corporate Events exhibitors.

Foundation days, award ceremonies and company-wide kickoffs need a different kind of build than a trade-show booth — large-format entrance structures, stages rated for thousands of attendees, and lighting and AV that carry a room. This is where Hayat Stage's event-production side takes over from stall fabrication.

Full AV & Lighting Production

Sound, lighting and LED walls run as one production unit, not separately contracted vendors.

A booth is built to be glanced into; an entrance structure for a foundation day or a dealer meet is built to be walked through, by a few hundred people at once — which is a structural question, not just a design one. We check wind and footfall load on arches and signage walls, plan cable routing so a red carpet stays trip-free, and on stage, move from a booth counter's light load to a deck rated for presenters, trophy tables and sometimes a performance, with truss overhead for moving-head lighting, line-array sound and an LED wall rigged in rather than dropped in. At Chennai Trade Centre in particular, that means designing the entrance and stage footprint to CTC's own compliance and layout rules from the start, rather than adjusting a generic plan after the fact.

The scope here runs wider than one type of event. We've built entrance arches and red-carpet experiences for foundation-day anniversaries, staged award ceremonies with cued lighting and trophy platforms, produced outdoor stages for town halls and all-hands kickoffs, and built interview sets and photo backdrops for launches and press days. Chennai's corporate base leans heavily on manufacturing, auto-ancillary and engineering firms alongside its growing tech sector, and dealer or partner meets — which combine a plenary stage with breakout zones — are a recurring format we design for here that's less common in a pure tech-sector city.

Where a stall is one contractor's job, a corporate stage pulls together a carpenter, a rigger, a lighting designer and a sound engineer — and coordinating four separate vendors' schedules into a single load-in window is its own project. We run stage, AV and lighting as one production unit: a single walkthrough of the CTC hall or your chosen venue, one rigging and cabling plan, one crew on site for load-in and load-out, and one point of contact if the run-of-show changes on the day. That matters more at a venue like CTC, where layout and compliance sign-off already has to happen before anything else — a single production team can move that conversation forward instead of three vendors waiting on each other.

Planning Ahead

Planning a Corporate Event in Chennai.

Start with three things: roughly how many people are attending, what format the event is (an award ceremony, a dealer or partner meet, a town hall), and a target date. Chennai Trade Centre, Nandambakkam is the primary large-venue option for corporate events in the city, and it has its own compliance and layout requirements that shape the entrance and stage design from the concept stage — so if CTC is even a possibility, flagging it early lets us design to its rules rather than retrofit a plan later.

Lead time matters most for large-format entrance structures and multi-truss stage rigs, since those need structural drawings signed off before load-in — the earlier your date and venue are locked, the more design time we have rather than rushed assembly. If your event brings together a plenary session with breakout zones, as many dealer and partner meets do, that's worth mentioning upfront too, since it changes the hall layout plan.

Share your attendee count, format and venue and we can put together a first concept and quote.
