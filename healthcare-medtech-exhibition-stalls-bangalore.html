FAQ

Healthcare & MedTech exhibition stalls in Bangalore, answered.

Can you accommodate medical equipment displays inside the stall?

Yes — we plan floor loading, power and clearances around the actual equipment you're bringing, whether that's a single unit or a simulated care environment.

Do you build private demo or consultation areas?

Yes — enclosed pods and semi-private zones are a common request for healthcare exhibitors and are built into the stall design from the start.
