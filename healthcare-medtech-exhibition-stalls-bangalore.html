Built For Healthcare & MedTech

Exhibition stalls engineered for Healthcare & MedTech exhibitors.

Healthcare and medical device exhibitors need more than a branded backdrop — they need a stall that can safely house equipment demos, imaging displays and, sometimes, simulated clinical environments. Hayat Stage builds around the hardware, not the other way around.

A

Equipment-Ready Structures

Reinforced flooring, dedicated power circuits and planned clearances built around ultrasound units, ICU beds and diagnostic hardware. We work from your equipment list and weights before we finalise the floor plan, not after.

B

Controlled Lighting & AV

Lighting and screen placement tuned so imaging and video displays read clearly under hall lighting, without glare or washout. Cable runs and AV points are routed into the structure at the design stage.

C

Private Demo Pods

Enclosed consultation or demo areas built into the stall for one-to-one clinical walkthroughs, away from show-floor noise. Sightlines and access points are planned around visitor flow, not bolted on afterward.

What "equipment-ready" actually means starts with the build, not the branding. A stall carrying an ultrasound cart or a simulated ICU bay needs a floor structure rated for the load, power circuits sized and positioned for the actual devices going on them, and cable management that keeps a demo area free of trip hazards during a full show day. At BIEC's larger halls, that also means designing the stall to fit hall compliance and load-in requirements from the start, rather than adjusting a generic booth after the fact. For exhibitors going into KTPO Whitefield's smaller-format shows, the same equipment-first thinking still applies, just scaled to a tighter footprint.

Bangalore's healthcare and medtech floor covers a wide range of exhibitor types, and we build for most of them. Device manufacturers bringing a single flagship product for a hands-on demo. Diagnostics companies showing imaging or lab equipment that needs a controlled, well-lit display area. Sterilization and PPE brands whose story is told through a clean, product-first counter rather than a wall of graphics. Pharma-adjacent exhibitors who need a professional meeting space more than a display. Bangalore's dual venue base suits this range well — BIEC for the larger device and diagnostics exhibitors running full-scale demos, KTPO Whitefield for smaller health-tech and diagnostics companies exhibiting alongside the city's broader tech and startup scene.

Finish matters more in this sector than in most. The people walking a healthcare or medtech stall — hospital procurement teams, distributors, clinical buyers — are evaluating your professionalism as much as your product, and a stall that looks improvised undercuts a device that's anything but. We build to a finish standard that reads as considered and credible under close inspection, not just from a distance across the hall, because that's the standard this audience is used to judging by.

When a stall needs to simulate part of a care environment — a mocked ICU bay, a diagnostic suite — we treat that as a build problem first: safe electrical loads for any powered equipment being shown, a floor and structure that can carry it, and finishes and enclosures that hold up to a full day of hands-on visitor traffic. We don't make claims about the equipment itself; that's for your team. Our job is making sure the structure around it holds up and looks right.

Planning Your Stall

Planning your Healthcare & MedTech stall in Bangalore.

A healthcare or medtech stall takes more upfront planning than a standard booth, mostly because of what's going inside it. Here's what helps us move fast once you reach out.

Your Equipment List

What's coming to the stall — models, approximate dimensions and weight — so we can plan floor structure and layout around it, not the other way around.

Power Requirements

Voltage, load and how many independent circuits your equipment needs, plus whether any of it needs a stable, dedicated supply.

Display & Screen Needs

Any imaging displays, monitors or video walls, and where they need to sit relative to your equipment and visitor sightlines.

Demo Or Consultation Space

Whether you need an enclosed pod for private walkthroughs, and roughly how many people it should hold.

Hall & Stall Details

Your allotted stall number and size once BIEC or KTPO Whitefield confirms it, plus your show dates.

Timing matters more for equipment-heavy stalls than for a simple modular counter — the structural and electrical planning takes longer than picking a backdrop and some furniture, so it helps to start the conversation as soon as your hall allocation and equipment list are settled, rather than waiting until show week. Scale also affects lead time: a large-format double-decker build for a major show at BIEC needs a longer runway than a modular stall going into KTPO Whitefield. Either way, the earlier we see your equipment and power requirements, the more the final structure is designed around them instead of adapted to them at the last minute.

FAQ

Healthcare & MedTech exhibition stalls in Bangalore, answered.

Can you accommodate medical equipment displays inside the stall?

Yes — we plan floor loading, power and clearances around the actual equipment you're bringing, whether that's a single diagnostic unit or a simulated care environment with multiple powered devices.

Do you build private demo or consultation areas?

Yes — enclosed pods and semi-private zones are a common request from healthcare and medtech exhibitors, and we build them into the stall design from the start rather than adding a curtain later.

Does BIEC's hall compliance affect how my stall is built?

Yes — BIEC applies its own hall compliance and load-in requirements for large-format and double-decker structures, and we fabricate to those requirements as standard for any healthcare or medtech build going into BIEC.

Can you plan dedicated power circuits for sensitive diagnostic equipment?

Yes — tell us the voltage, load and number of circuits your equipment needs and we plan the electrical layout around it, including separating circuits where your devices call for it.

Is KTPO Whitefield a good fit for a smaller health-tech or diagnostics booth?

Often, yes — KTPO Whitefield suits modular and mid-size stalls well, which fits many diagnostics and health-tech exhibitors showing a smaller equipment footprint alongside Bangalore's broader tech and startup exhibitions.

How much does a healthcare or medtech stall cost in Bangalore?

It depends on size, format, finish and how much equipment integration the stall needs — equipment-heavy builds with reinforced flooring and dedicated circuits cost more than a simple display counter. Share your hall size and equipment list and we'll send an itemised quote.
