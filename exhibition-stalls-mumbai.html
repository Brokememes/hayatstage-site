Built For Mumbai's Shows

From consumer expos to finance summits.

Mumbai is India's financial capital, its biggest media and entertainment market, and one of the country's largest consumer and retail hubs — and its exhibition calendar runs across all three. A single month on the Mumbai show floor can move from a mass-market consumer expo with heavy walk-in traffic, to a closed-door BFSI or asset-management summit, to a brand activation built to be photographed and shared. Hayat Stage builds for all three without treating any one of them as the default and the others as an afterthought.

A city with three different exhibition identities

Consumer and retail exhibitors come to Mumbai for scale — dense halls, heavy footfall and a stall that has to hold its ground and its finish for the full run of the show. Banking, finance and insurance exhibitors come for the city's concentration of BFSI head offices, and tend to treat their stand as a quieter, more considered brand moment rather than a loud trade-show presence. Media, entertainment and consumer-brand marketers come for Mumbai's position as India's content and advertising capital, where a stall or activation set often has to work as hard on camera as it does on the show floor. The venue, format and finish we recommend start from which of these three worlds your show sits in.

Same process, Mumbai specifics built in

Whichever of those three shows you're building for, the process itself doesn't change: a 3D concept for sign-off before fabrication starts, an on-ground install crew for setup and dismantling, and an itemised quote with no surprise costs after hall possession. What changes in Mumbai is what our local crew already knows going in — NESCO's Goregaon access windows versus BKC's tighter service-lift and load-in constraints, and which finish standard a given hall or venue actually expects.

Venues We Build For

Wherever your show is held in Mumbai.

Mumbai exhibitors are usually choosing between two very different venues, and the right build depends on which one you're in. NESCO suits scale — major trade shows, dense hall traffic and stands that need real structural headroom. Jio World Convention Centre suits polish — a corporate and finance-sector audience that expects a quieter, design-forward finish over sheer footprint. We scope each differently from the first call.

Bombay Exhibition Centre (NESCO), Goregaon

Large-format and double-decker stalls for major trade shows, built for NESCO's dense halls and heavy walk-through traffic. The default choice for a big consumer, industrial or manufacturing show that needs real footprint.

Jio World Convention Centre, BKC

Premium finish stalls for corporate and finance-sector events, scoped for JWCC's tighter service-lift access and higher finish expectations. The right choice when the brief is a design-forward brand moment rather than a trade-show floor presence.

Planning Ahead

Planning your stall in Mumbai.

The first decision is which Mumbai venue you're building for — a NESCO trade-show stand and a JWCC corporate build are different projects with different lead times, formats and finish standards. Telling us your venue and show dates early lets us scope the right build from the first call.

01

Tell Us Your Venue

NESCO Goregaon or Jio World Convention Centre at BKC — the venue shapes whether this is a large-format trade-show stand or a premium corporate build.

02

Share Hall Size & Position

Your allotted stall dimensions and hall position let us size the structure correctly and plan load-in logistics for that specific venue.

03

Send Brand & Industry Brief

Logo files, brand guidelines and a short note on your industry — tech demo, healthcare compliance, retail merchandising or a corporate stage — so the design brief starts in the right place.

04

Confirm Show Dates

NESCO and BKC run on different access and move-in windows; confirmed dates let us plan fabrication and the Mumbai install crew around the right schedule.

A large-format NESCO stand needs a longer fabrication runway to match its structural scale, so we get moving on drawings as soon as your hall allocation is confirmed. A JWCC or BKC corporate build can sometimes move faster once the concept is locked, but its premium finish standards leave less room to improvise on-site than a typical trade-show floor.

FAQ

Mumbai exhibition stalls, answered.

Which Mumbai venues do you build for?

We build for any Mumbai venue, including Bombay Exhibition Centre (NESCO) in Goregaon and Jio World Convention Centre at BKC, matched to each venue's format and compliance requirements.

How do I choose between NESCO and Jio World Convention Centre?

It comes down to your show and audience. NESCO suits large-format trade shows with heavy consumer footfall and needs headroom for double-decker structures. JWCC at BKC suits corporate, finance-sector and premium brand events where a tighter, design-forward footprint and a more restrained finish matter more than raw scale.

How much lead time do you need for a Mumbai build?

For a large-format NESCO stand, we start as soon as your hall allocation and stall position are confirmed, since bigger footprints take longer to finalise structurally. A JWCC or BKC corporate build can sometimes move on a shorter runway, but earlier confirmation of the brief and finish requirements matters more there than raw lead time.

What stall formats do you build in Mumbai?

Everything from compact modular booths to large-format and double-decker stands at NESCO, plus premium corporate stages, activation sets and conference environments at venues like Jio World Convention Centre and BKC hotels.

How much does an exhibition stall cost in Mumbai?

Pricing depends on venue, size, format and finish level — a NESCO trade-show stand and a premium JWCC corporate stand sit at different budgets. Share your hall size, venue and show dates and we'll send an itemised Mumbai quote.

Which industries do you build for in Mumbai?

Five verticals: Technology & SaaS, Healthcare & MedTech, Energy & Industrial Manufacturing, Consumer, Retail & Travel, and Corporate Events & Conferences — each with format and finish adjusted to that industry's Mumbai audience.

Can you handle high-footfall consumer expos?

Yes — our structures are designed and rated for the heavy consumer and B2C traffic typical of Mumbai's largest exhibitions, particularly at NESCO.

Do you handle installation and dismantling at Mumbai venues?

Yes — our on-ground Mumbai install crew handles setup, live show-day support and breakdown, including hall handover, at both NESCO and Jio World Convention Centre.
