Delhi hosts India's largest national and international healthcare and medtech exhibitions, most of them at Pragati Maidan's Bharat Mandapam (IICC) complex. That scale changes the brief: custom, modular, double-decker and full pavilion-format builds are all common here, and IICC applies its own structural and protocol requirements to each — particularly around access, security movement and load-in scheduling for larger builds. We fabricate to those requirements as standard, and for pavilion-format work, we plan the structural engineering early enough to clear IICC's process without last-minute redesign.

The exhibitor mix at Delhi's national-scale healthcare shows is wide. Large device manufacturers running full product lines and live demos. Diagnostics and imaging companies. Sterilization and PPE brands. Pharma-adjacent exhibitors meeting institutional and government buyers rather than walk-in visitors. International exhibitors using a country pavilion format to present a coordinated group presence rather than individual booths. We build for all of these formats, from a single equipment-focused stall to a larger multi-exhibitor pavilion structure.

A stall at a Delhi national or international healthcare show is often being evaluated against exhibitors from dozens of other countries and companies in the same hall, in front of an audience that includes government health bodies, institutional procurement teams and international delegations. That's a higher bar for finish than a regional show, and it's not one you get to on a generic template. We build Delhi healthcare and medtech stalls to hold up to that side-by-side comparison — considered detailing, consistent branding and a structure that reads as intentional rather than assembled.

For any build simulating a clinical or care environment at scale — a full pavilion mock-up, a multi-station diagnostics display — our scope stays on the structure: correctly planned power for every powered element, load-bearing capacity for the equipment and any upper-deck weight, and protocol-compliant access and egress for IICC. What the equipment does clinically, and any regulatory claims about it, are for your team to represent.

Planning Your Stall

Planning your Healthcare & MedTech stall in Delhi.

A healthcare or medtech stall at a national or international show takes more upfront planning than a standard booth. Here's what helps us move fast once you reach out.

Your Equipment List

Models, approximate dimensions and weight for everything coming to the stall, including any pavilion or multi-exhibitor components.

Power Requirements

Voltage, load and how many independent circuits your equipment needs across the full structure.

Display & Screen Needs

Any imaging displays, monitors or video walls, and where they need to sit relative to your equipment and visitor sightlines.

Demo Or Consultation Space

Whether you need an enclosed pod for institutional, government or delegation meetings, and roughly how many people it should hold.

IICC Stall Details

Your allotted stall, hall or pavilion allocation once Bharat Mandapam confirms it, plus your show dates.

Bharat Mandapam applies structural and protocol requirements that are stricter than a typical hall — particularly for pavilion-format builds, multi-exhibitor structures, or any stall involving government or institutional visitors with their own access protocols. Because of that, it's worth starting the structural conversation earlier for a Delhi build than you might for a smaller regional show, especially if you're planning a double-decker or pavilion-format stall. The larger and more complex the format, the more runway the structural design and IICC approval process needs before load-in.

FAQ

Healthcare & MedTech exhibition stalls in Delhi, answered.

Can you accommodate medical equipment displays inside the stall?

Yes — we plan floor loading, power and clearances around the actual equipment you're bringing, from a single diagnostic unit to a full pavilion-format display.

Do you build private demo or consultation areas?

Yes — enclosed pods are common for exhibitors meeting government health bodies, institutional buyers or international delegations away from the open floor, and we design them into the stall from the start.

What does Bharat Mandapam's protocol requirement mean for my stall?

IICC applies structural and protocol requirements around access, security movement and load-in scheduling, particularly for larger and pavilion-format builds. We fabricate and schedule to those requirements as standard.

Can you build a full pavilion-format stall, not just a standalone booth?

Yes — pavilion-format and multi-exhibitor structures are a regular part of our Delhi scope, especially for national and international shows at Bharat Mandapam.

Can you plan dedicated power circuits for sensitive diagnostic equipment?

Yes — tell us the voltage, load and number of circuits your equipment needs and we plan the electrical layout around it, including separating circuits where your devices call for it.

How much does a healthcare or medtech stall cost in Delhi?

It depends on size, format, finish and how much equipment or pavilion structure the stall needs. Share your hall size and equipment list and we'll send an itemised quote.
