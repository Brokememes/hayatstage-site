Built For Consumer & Retail

Exhibition stalls engineered for Consumer & Retail exhibitors.

Consumer, retail and travel brands need a stall that performs under heavy foot traffic and photographs well from every angle. Hayat Stage builds retail-grade merchandising into the structure — racks, product walls and reception counters — finished to a standard that holds up to a full day of visitors.

Chennai Trade Centre in Nandambakkam is the city's primary venue for consumer, apparel and lifestyle exhibitions, and it runs its own compliance and layout process — stall drawings, structural sign-off and hall-specific rules that shape what you can build and where. We fabricate to CTC's requirements as a matter of course, which means fewer surprises at move-in and a stall that clears inspection the first time.

Format at CTC is genuinely flexible: a compact modular unit works well for a regional distributor testing a new market, while a bigger custom or double-decker build suits a national retail chain or travel brand anchoring a hall position. The right call comes down to your footprint, your product range and how much of a hosting or meeting space you need on an upper deck.

Merchandising planning starts with your actual product mix, not a generic layout. We size racking and shelving to the SKU count you're actually bringing, place a checkout or POS counter where it won't choke visitor flow through the aisle, and keep a stock and staff area tucked behind the display line so restocking doesn't interrupt browsing.

CTC's exhibition halls mix natural light near the entrances with standard hall lighting deeper inside, so we plan lighting and backdrop placement stall by stall rather than assuming a single lighting setup works everywhere — the goal is a product wall and reception area that photograph consistently regardless of where in the hall you land.

Shows at CTC typically run across several days with visitor volumes that build through the run, so joinery, flooring and display fixtures are specified to handle continuous handling — leaning, browsing, restocking — without the finish degrading before the final day.

Planning Ahead

Planning your Consumer & Retail stall in Chennai.

Because CTC runs its own compliance and layout sign-off process, the projects that go smoothest are the ones where we know the venue rules and your footprint early — bring us in as soon as your hall position at Chennai Trade Centre is confirmed and we'll fold the approval step into the build schedule rather than racing it at the end.

01

Confirm Your CTC Footprint

Hall, stall dimensions and position within Chennai Trade Centre — this decides whether modular, custom or double-decker fits.

02

Share Your Product Range

A SKU list or product category breakdown so racking, shelving and product-wall spacing get sized correctly from the concept stage.

03

Send Brand Assets

Logo files, brand colours and any existing show photography so the stand photographs consistently with your other marketing.

04

Flag CTC Compliance Needs

Tell us about any double-decker or large-format plans early so structural drawings and CTC sign-off happen well ahead of move-in.

Custom and double-decker builds at CTC need more design and fabrication runway than a modular unit — structural drawings typically have to clear the venue's compliance process before fabrication can start, and we treat that as a scheduled step rather than an afterthought. A modular or mid-size custom stand can generally move faster, which suits a first-time CTC exhibitor or a brand testing a smaller footprint before committing to a bigger presence.
