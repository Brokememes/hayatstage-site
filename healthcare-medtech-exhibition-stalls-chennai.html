Built For Healthcare & MedTech

Exhibition stalls engineered for Healthcare & MedTech exhibitors.

Healthcare and medical device exhibitors need more than a branded backdrop — they need a stall that can safely house equipment demos, imaging displays and, sometimes, simulated clinical environments. Hayat Stage builds around the hardware, not the other way around.

A

Equipment-Ready Structures

Reinforced flooring, dedicated power circuits and planned clearances built around ultrasound units, ICU beds and diagnostic hardware — fabricated to fit Chennai Trade Centre's hall layout and compliance requirements from the start.

B

Controlled Lighting & AV

Lighting and screen placement tuned so imaging and video displays read clearly on CTC's show floor, with cable runs and AV points routed into the structure rather than run loose across walkways.

C

Private Demo Pods

Enclosed consultation or demo areas built into the stall for one-to-one walkthroughs — useful when an equipment demo needs a quieter, more controlled space than the open hall floor.

Chennai Trade Centre in Nandambakkam is the primary venue for the city's exhibition circuit, and it's a single, well-understood building rather than a sprawling multi-hall campus — which simplifies load-in logistics but still means every custom, modular or double-decker stall has to be fabricated to CTC's specific compliance and layout requirements. For healthcare and medtech exhibitors, that means getting the floor plan, power layout and any double-decker structural details checked against CTC's requirements early, rather than discovering a mismatch during load-in week.

Tamil Nadu's manufacturing base means a meaningful share of Chennai's healthcare and medtech exhibitors are device makers and component suppliers showing product built or assembled close to home, alongside diagnostics companies and sterilization and PPE brands presenting to hospital and distributor buyers. That mix means stalls here often need to do double duty — part product showcase, part manufacturing credibility statement — which is a different brief from a pure marketing booth.

Buyers on a manufacturing-adjacent show floor tend to scrutinise build quality instinctively — it's the same lens they'd apply to a factory line or a finished product. A healthcare or medtech stall that looks thrown together undercuts that credibility fast. We finish Chennai builds to a standard that reads as deliberate and well-made up close, not just from across the hall, because that's the standard this audience already applies to everything else they're evaluating that day.

Where a stall needs to simulate part of a clinical or lab environment, our responsibility is the structure — safe, correctly rated power for any equipment being shown, a floor built to carry it, and finishes and enclosures that hold up through a multi-day show. We don't speak to the equipment's function or any regulatory status; that's your team's story to tell.

Planning Your Stall

Planning your Healthcare & MedTech stall in Chennai.

A healthcare or medtech stall takes more upfront planning than a standard booth. Here's what helps us move fast once you reach out.

Your Equipment List

Models, approximate dimensions and weight for everything coming to the stall, so we can plan the floor structure around it.

Power Requirements

Voltage, load and how many independent circuits your equipment needs.

Display & Screen Needs

Any imaging displays, monitors or video walls, and where they should sit relative to the equipment.

Demo Or Consultation Space

Whether you need an enclosed pod for private walkthroughs, and roughly how many people it should hold.

CTC Stall Details

Your allotted stall number and size once Chennai Trade Centre confirms it, plus your show dates.

Chennai Trade Centre's single-building layout at Nandambakkam makes load-in logistics comparatively straightforward compared with a multi-hall campus, but CTC still has its own compliance and layout requirements for custom and double-decker structures, and those are worth checking against your equipment plan early rather than at load-in. If your stall needs to combine a manufacturing or OEM story with a live equipment demo, tell us early too — it changes how we split the floor plan between display space and demo space, and that's much easier to plan in before the design is locked than to retrofit afterward.

FAQ

Healthcare & MedTech exhibition stalls in Chennai, answered.

Can you accommodate medical equipment displays inside the stall?

Yes — we plan floor loading, power and clearances around the actual equipment you're bringing, whether that's a single diagnostic unit or a simulated care environment.

Do you build private demo or consultation areas?

Yes — enclosed pods and semi-private zones are a common request for healthcare and medtech exhibitors, and we build them into the stall design from the start.

Does Chennai Trade Centre have specific compliance requirements for healthcare stalls?

Yes — CTC applies its own compliance and layout requirements to custom, modular and double-decker structures, and we fabricate to those requirements as standard for any Nandambakkam build.

Can you plan dedicated power circuits for sensitive diagnostic equipment?

Yes — tell us the voltage, load and number of circuits your equipment needs and we plan the electrical layout around it, including separating circuits where your devices call for it.

Can the stall combine a manufacturing or OEM story with a live equipment demo?

Yes — tell us early and we'll split the floor plan between display and demo space accordingly; it's a common brief for Chennai's device and component manufacturers.

How much does a healthcare or medtech stall cost in Chennai?

It depends on size, format, finish and how much equipment integration the stall needs. Share your hall size and equipment list and we'll send an itemised quote.
