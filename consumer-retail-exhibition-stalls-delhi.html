Built For Consumer & Retail

Exhibition stalls engineered for Consumer & Retail exhibitors.

Consumer, retail and travel brands need a stall that performs under heavy foot traffic and photographs well from every angle. Hayat Stage builds retail-grade merchandising into the structure — racks, product walls and reception counters — finished to a standard that holds up to a full day of visitors.

Delhi's major consumer and retail exhibitions run through Bharat Mandapam, the redeveloped International Exhibition-cum-Convention Centre (IICC) built on the Pragati Maidan grounds. It's a larger, more structured venue than most Indian exhibition halls, with its own protocol and structural requirements that apply from the first drawing — which means a stall built for IICC has to clear that process cleanly rather than adapt on the fly during move-in.

IICC's scale opens up format options that smaller venues don't: alongside custom, modular and double-decker builds, it hosts full pavilion-format presences for national and government-adjacent retail or travel exhibitors who need a larger, more ceremonial footprint. Most consumer and retail brands sit somewhere between a modular in-line unit for a focused product launch and a custom double-decker for a bigger brand statement.

Merchandising has to work at IICC's scale too — racking and shelving sized to your actual SKU range, a POS or checkout counter placed so it doesn't obstruct the wider aisles the venue is built around, and back-of-house stock space that keeps restocking and staff breaks out of visitor sightlines during peak hours.

IICC's halls are built with high ceilings and structured lighting rigs, which is an advantage for photography if you plan for it — strong backdrop walls, consistent brand-colour lighting and a clear sightline into the product wall all read well against the venue's more formal architecture, whether the shot is for a press visit or a product-launch reel.

Shows at IICC typically run across several days with structured move-in and move-out windows tied to the venue's protocol requirements, so we specify finishes and joinery to survive continuous handling across the full run rather than just looking sharp at the ribbon-cutting.

Planning Ahead

Planning your Consumer & Retail stall in Delhi.

Bharat Mandapam's structural and protocol requirements are more formal than most exhibition venues, so the earlier we know your format ambitions — modular, custom, double-decker or full pavilion — the more runway we have to get drawings through IICC's process and avoid a compressed build schedule.

01

Confirm Hall & Footprint

Hall, stall dimensions and format ambition at Bharat Mandapam — pavilion-format needs are worth flagging as early as possible.

02

Share Your Product Range

A SKU list or product category breakdown so racking, shelving and product-wall spacing get sized correctly from the start.

03

Send Brand Assets

Logo files, brand colours and reference imagery so the stall photographs consistently against IICC's formal architecture.

04

Flag IICC Protocol Needs

Government-adjacent or ceremonial exhibitors should tell us early — protocol requirements at IICC can shape the build timeline.

Custom, double-decker and pavilion-format builds at Bharat Mandapam need meaningfully more design and fabrication lead time than a modular unit — structural drawings typically need to clear IICC's protocol and compliance process before fabrication starts, and we schedule that step in rather than treating it as a late-stage formality. A modular or mid-size custom stand can generally move on a shorter runway, which suits brands testing a first Delhi presence before committing to a larger format.

FAQ

Consumer & Retail exhibition stalls in Delhi, answered.

Do you build pavilion-format stalls for Bharat Mandapam (IICC)?

Yes — alongside custom, modular and double-decker formats, we build pavilion-format stalls at IICC fabricated to its structural and protocol requirements.

Is Pragati Maidan the same venue as Bharat Mandapam?

Bharat Mandapam (IICC) is the redeveloped exhibition complex built on the Pragati Maidan grounds. We fabricate to its current structural and protocol requirements for whichever hall or format your show uses.

Do you build in product merchandising like racks and shelving?

Can you fit a checkout or POS counter into the layout?
