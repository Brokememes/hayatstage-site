Built For Consumer & Retail

Exhibition stalls engineered for Consumer & Retail exhibitors.

Consumer, retail and travel brands need a stall that performs under heavy foot traffic and photographs well from every angle. Hayat Stage builds retail-grade merchandising into the structure — racks, product walls and reception counters — finished to a standard that holds up to a full day of visitors.

Gurgaon works differently from a dedicated exhibition city. There's no single large-format trade-show hall here — most Consumer & Retail work happens inside convention hotel ballrooms and corporate-park venues, built around a product launch, a channel-partner showcase or a brand activation rather than a multi-day public expo. "Heavy foot traffic" in this context usually means a concentrated evening or single-day crowd of guests, press and buyers moving through a launch set, not a weekend of walk-in shoppers.

That changes the brief. A hotel ballroom launch set is closer to a stage build than a classic exhibition stall — a hero product display, a branded backdrop for press photography, and often a compact retail or sampling counter rather than full racking. A corporate-park brand showcase leans more toward booths and demo counters for a professional, walk-through audience. We size the format to the venue and the event, not to a standard exhibition template.

Where product merchandising is part of the brief — a retail brand sampling or selling on-site — we still build in proper racking, product-wall spacing and a checkout or POS counter, scaled to a hotel or corporate-park footprint rather than a hall-sized one, and positioned so it doesn't interrupt guest flow through a ballroom or lobby.

Photography matters even more here, because a Gurgaon launch event is often built specifically to generate press and social coverage. Lighting, backdrop walls and a clean brand-colour palette are treated as core to the brief — the shot from the launch needs to work as a standalone image, not just as a record of attendance.

Because Gurgaon sits inside the wider NCR region, we also run the same crew across Gurgaon, Delhi and neighbouring NCR venues for brands doing a multi-stop campaign — a Delhi exhibition stall and a Gurgaon corporate launch in the same cycle, handled without switching vendors mid-campaign.

Planning Ahead

Planning your Consumer & Retail build in Gurgaon.

Because most Gurgaon Consumer & Retail work is a hotel or corporate-park event rather than a standard exhibition-hall booking, the venue itself brings its own rules — ballroom load-in times, freight-elevator access, noise and fire restrictions that vary hotel to hotel. Telling us the venue as early as possible lets us plan around its specific constraints instead of discovering them at load-in.

01

Confirm The Venue

Which hotel ballroom or corporate park, and its access, load-in and space rules — these vary far more than a standard exhibition hall.

02

Define The Event Format

Product launch, channel showcase or retail activation — this decides whether you need a stage set, a booth, or both.

03

Send Brand Assets

Logo files, brand colours and any hero product imagery so the launch set is ready for press and social coverage on the night.

04

Flag Multi-City Needs

If Gurgaon is one stop on a wider NCR or Delhi campaign, tell us early so one crew can plan the full run together.

A hotel ballroom build usually moves faster than a full exhibition stall — there's no venue compliance process to clear the way there is at a dedicated exhibition centre — but load-in windows tend to be shorter and stricter, so we still need the concept locked and materials ready well before the event date. If your campaign also includes a stop at Pragati Maidan / Bharat Mandapam in Delhi or another NCR venue, we plan both builds together as one project rather than treating them as separate jobs.

FAQ

Consumer & Retail exhibition stalls in Gurgaon, answered.

Do you build product-launch sets in Gurgaon convention hotels?

Yes — launch sets, booths and stage builds inside Gurgaon's convention hotels and corporate parks are a core part of what we do here, alongside standard exhibition stalls.

Can one crew cover a Gurgaon event and a Delhi NCR venue?

Yes — we cover Gurgaon alongside Delhi and neighbouring NCR venues as one crew, which is useful for multi-stop campaigns that hit more than one city or venue in the same run.

Do you build in product merchandising like racks and shelving?

Yes — retail display fixtures, shelving depth and product-wall spacing are designed into the build from the concept stage, matched to your actual product range.

Can you fit a checkout or POS counter into a hotel launch set?

Yes — where a hotel or corporate-park venue allows retail sales or sampling on-site, we plan point-of-sale counters and stock space into the layout so they don't interrupt guest flow.
