Healthcare and medical device exhibitors need more than a branded backdrop — they need a stall that can safely house equipment demos, imaging displays and, sometimes, simulated clinical environments. Hayat Stage builds around the hardware, not the other way around.

Mumbai's healthcare and medtech shows split across two very different environments, and the build has to match. NESCO's Bombay Exhibition Centre in Goregaon hosts the city's larger-format trade shows, where double-decker structures and heavier equipment displays are common and the floor plan needs to account for genuine load and power demand. Jio World Convention Centre in BKC serves a different kind of event — smaller, more curated, and finished to a standard that fits a premium commercial address. We build for both, but we don't use the same playbook for each: a NESCO hall build is engineered first for scale and equipment; a BKC build is engineered first for finish and precision.

The range of exhibitors we see on Mumbai's healthcare and medtech floor is broad. Device manufacturers running live equipment demos. Diagnostics and imaging companies whose entire pitch depends on a screen or a scanner reading clearly under hall lighting. Sterilization and PPE brands built around a clean, product-first counter rather than heavy signage. And a growing number of exhibitors using the Mumbai circuit — particularly BKC — to present to hospital networks, distributors and investors rather than walk-in show-floor traffic, which changes what the stall needs to do.

Mumbai is India's commercial and financial capital, and the audience walking a BKC-hosted healthcare event often includes procurement committees, institutional buyers and investors who are used to a certain standard of finish in every other room they walk into that week. A stall that looks assembled rather than designed reads badly in that context, regardless of how strong the product is. We finish healthcare and medtech stalls in Mumbai to hold up under that kind of close, professional scrutiny — clean joinery, consistent branding and lighting that flatters the equipment rather than competing with it.

For any build that simulates a care environment — a mocked consultation room, a diagnostics bay — our scope is the structure and the environment around it: safe power for whatever's plugged in, a floor that can carry it, and finishes that read as clean and considered. What the equipment itself does, and any claims about it, stay with your team.

Planning Your Stall

Planning your Healthcare & MedTech stall in Mumbai.

A healthcare or medtech stall needs more upfront information than a standard booth. Here's what helps us move quickly once you reach out.

Equipment List

Models, approximate dimensions and weight for everything coming to the stall, so floor structure and layout are planned around it.

Power Requirements

Voltage, load and any dedicated or backup power needs for the equipment you're bringing.

Display & Screen Needs

Imaging monitors or video walls, and where they need to sit relative to your equipment and visitor sightlines.

Demo Or Consultation Space

Whether you need a private pod for hospital, distributor or investor meetings, and roughly how many people it should hold.

Venue & Stall Details

Your NESCO or Jio World Convention Centre allocation, size and show dates once confirmed.

Which of Mumbai's two main venues you're building for changes the planning timeline. A large-format or double-decker stall at NESCO's Goregaon halls needs a longer structural and electrical planning runway than a smaller, premium-finish build at Jio World Convention Centre in BKC, where the space is more contained but the finish expectations are higher. Either way, start the conversation as soon as your hall or room allocation and equipment list are confirmed — the electrical and structural planning takes longer than sourcing furniture and graphics, and it's the part that's hardest to compress close to show dates.

FAQ

Healthcare & MedTech exhibition stalls in Mumbai, answered.

Can you accommodate medical equipment displays inside the stall?

Yes — we plan floor loading, power and clearances around the actual equipment you're bringing, whether it's a single diagnostic unit at a NESCO hall stall or a full simulated care environment in a BKC presentation space.

Do you build private demo or consultation areas?

Yes — enclosed pods and semi-private zones are a regular request from healthcare and medtech exhibitors meeting hospital groups, distributors or investors, and we design them into the stall from the start.

What's different about building for NESCO versus Jio World Convention Centre?

NESCO's Goregaon halls suit large-format and double-decker stalls built for scale and heavier equipment; Jio World Convention Centre in BKC is a more contained, premium-finish environment suited to corporate and investor-facing presentations. We plan the structure and finish differently for each.

Can you plan dedicated power circuits for sensitive diagnostic equipment?

Yes — share the voltage, load and number of circuits your equipment needs and we build the electrical layout around it, including separate circuits where your devices call for it.

Does the stall finish matter more for a BKC event than a trade-show hall?

It tends to, yes. BKC audiences are used to a premium commercial standard in every other room they visit, and a stall that looks assembled rather than designed reads badly in that context — so we build to a higher finish standard for BKC-style events by default.

How much does a healthcare or medtech stall cost in Mumbai?

It depends on size, venue, format and how much equipment integration is involved — a large NESCO double-decker costs differently to a smaller premium BKC build. Share your hall size and equipment list and we'll send an itemised quote.
