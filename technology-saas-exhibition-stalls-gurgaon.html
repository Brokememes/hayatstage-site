Built For Technology & SaaS

Exhibition stalls engineered for Technology & SaaS exhibitors.

Technology and software exhibitors live or die on the demo. Hayat Stage builds booths designed around working screens, live product walkthroughs and clean, brand-accurate signage — plus the stages behind partner kickoffs, developer conferences and operations meetings.

What the demo actually needs

Most of our Gurgaon tech-vertical work isn't a trade-show booth at all — it's a working demo built into a hotel ballroom or corporate-park venue for a product launch, sales kickoff or ops meeting, which changes what the demo needs. There's no hall infrastructure to lean on, so power runs, networking and sightlines to the demo station have to be planned into the stage or booth build itself, alongside signage that carries your brand system accurately in a space that wasn't designed for exhibitors.

Who this vertical covers

Gurgaon's tech and SaaS base is largely corporate headquarters and regional offices rather than trade-show exhibitors, so this vertical here covers SaaS and enterprise-software companies running partner and customer events, hardware and electronics brands doing product launches, and security-technology companies staging brand or channel events — all built around a live demo or product reveal rather than a public exhibition floor.

Booths — and the stages behind them

This is where the stages behind partner kickoffs, developer conferences and operations meetings are the main event rather than an add-on. Gurgaon's Cyber City and DLF business-park corridor houses a dense concentration of tech and SaaS regional and India headquarters, and a large share of our work here is the LED backdrop, stage set and branded environment for that company's internal or partner event — not a stall build at all.

Because Gurgaon doesn't have its own major exhibition hall, we build in convention hotels and corporate-park venues across the city, and for exhibitors running a multi-stop campaign, the same crew covers Gurgaon alongside Delhi and neighbouring NCR venues as one production run rather than separate bookings. That matters if your event calendar has a Gurgaon stop and a Delhi or Noida stop in the same week.

Planning Ahead

Planning Your Tech Stall In Gurgaon.

Without a dedicated exhibition hall, most Gurgaon tech-vertical projects start with a venue conversation, not a hall-size conversation — here's what's worth sorting out first.

Lead Time

A hotel-ballroom stage build or product-launch set typically needs less structural lead time than a trade-show stall, but venue booking, AV load-in windows and brand-asset sign-off tend to be the real constraints — confirm your venue and date early.

What To Bring To The First Call

Your brand guidelines, what's being demoed or launched and its power/space and AV needs, and your venue if it's already booked — a hotel's load-in rules and ceiling rigging points affect the design more than most exhibitors expect.

Gurgaon & NCR Logistics

If your campaign has stops in Gurgaon and Delhi or Noida in the same week, tell us upfront — we run it as one crew and one production schedule across NCR rather than treating each stop as a separate booking.
