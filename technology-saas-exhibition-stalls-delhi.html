Built For Technology & SaaS

Exhibition stalls engineered for Technology & SaaS exhibitors.

Technology and software exhibitors live or die on the demo. Hayat Stage builds booths designed around working screens, live product walkthroughs and clean, brand-accurate signage — plus the stages behind partner kickoffs, developer conferences and operations meetings.

What the demo actually needs

At the scale IICC operates on, a working demo isn't a nice-to-have detail — it has to survive a hall with heavier footfall, more competing stands and, on many shows, official delegations and formal walk-throughs where the stall needs to hold up under scrutiny. We plan power runs, sightlines to the demo station and brand-accurate signage into the concept from day one, so the booth photographs and performs the same on hour one and hour eight of a national-scale show.

Who this vertical covers

Delhi's technology exhibitor base is genuinely broad — SaaS platforms, hardware and electronics brands, security and anti-counterfeiting technology, and industrial test-and-measurement equipment all show up at IICC, often in the same hall on the same week. We've built for exactly that spread, including a brand-protection technology company's product-authentication displays and an antenna and test-systems specialist's instrumentation stand — different products, same requirement for a stall that gets out of the product's way.

Booths — and the stages behind them

IICC hosts genuinely large-scale national and international shows, and because Delhi is the seat of national industry associations and many pan-India company headquarters, the events behind our exhibition-stall work here run bigger too — partner kickoffs and ops meetings for companies whose India leadership is based in or travels through Delhi. Same fabrication and production team on both sides.

Pragati Maidan and Bharat Mandapam, run as the IICC complex, is built for custom, modular, double-decker and full pavilion-format stalls, and every one of them gets fabricated to IICC's structural and protocol requirements — a stricter and more formal approval path than most venues, reflecting the scale and profile of the shows it hosts. We build that compliance path into the schedule from the first concept, rather than treating it as a late-stage hurdle.

Planning Ahead

Planning Your Tech Stall In Delhi.

IICC shows run at a scale and formality that's different from most other venues, and Delhi's protocol and structural approval requirements are worth planning around early rather than discovering at hall possession.

Lead Time

Custom, double-decker and pavilion-format stalls at IICC need longer design and structural-approval lead time than a modular stand elsewhere — start the conversation as soon as your hall and stall allocation is confirmed.

What To Bring To The First Call

Your brand guidelines, what's being demoed and its power/space needs, and your IICC hall size and stall number. If your show involves delegation visits or heightened security protocol, flag it early — it affects the structural approval path.

Delhi Logistics

IICC's structural and protocol approval process is more formal than most venues we build for, so we factor its documentation and sign-off timeline into the schedule from the first concept, not as a late add-on.
