Bangalore's exhibition floor looks the way it does because of what the city actually builds. It's India's best-known technology and electronics hub, home to a dense concentration of software companies, hardware and semiconductor design firms, and global capability centres — and that shows up directly in the exhibitor mix at any major show here. The city also carries a genuine aerospace and precision-engineering base, tied to the aviation and defence-manufacturing corridor that runs through Karnataka, so trade fairs regularly host exhibitors bringing real machined components and industrial hardware, not just brochures and banners. Layer in Bangalore's startup ecosystem — one of the most active in the country — and you get show floors that mix SaaS demo booths, hardware and electronics counters, industrial exhibitors with heavier structural needs, and startup pitch-day kiosks, sometimes in the same hall the same week.

"The same flagship service, delivered locally" isn't a slogan — it describes how a Bangalore project actually runs. Design and fabrication are handled by a team based in the city, working from the same 3D-concept-to-approval process used on every Hayat Stage project nationally, so what you sign off on is what gets built, not a simplified regional version of it. Materials — ply, laminate, vinyl, structural framing, LED and signage components — are sourced through a Bangalore-based vendor network, which keeps production timelines independent of inter-city freight delays. On show days, a local install crew handles setup, live floor support and full dismantling, and that crew knows BIEC's and KTPO's loading docks, access windows and hall rules from repeat experience rather than a one-off site visit.

Choosing between BIEC and KTPO mostly comes down to your show, not preference. BIEC — Bangalore International Exhibition Centre, on Tumkur Road — hosts the city's large-format national and international trade shows, and this is where double-decker stands, large custom pavilions and heavier structural builds happen. Exhibiting at BIEC, especially from an island or larger corner position, means hall compliance becomes part of the build timeline rather than paperwork tacked on at the end: structural drawings, electrical load approvals and fire-safety sign-off typically need to clear before fabrication is finalised, so we plan that approval track alongside design from day one. KTPO in Whitefield sits inside Bangalore's tech corridor itself, and tends to host mid-size, corporate-facing and tech-industry exhibitions and conferences. Modular and mid-size custom stalls are the norm there, turnaround windows are generally shorter, and a large share of exhibitors are based five minutes away rather than flying in equipment and crew. If your show is at BIEC, budget for the compliance timeline; if it's at KTPO, a modular or lighter custom build usually fits both the venue and the schedule.

That range of exhibitor types is exactly why Hayat Stage runs five dedicated industry verticals rather than one generic stall template — a SaaS company's demo booth, a diagnostics brand's equipment display and a manufacturing exhibitor's heavy-duty stand are different design problems with different starting points. Planning Ahead

Planning Your Stall in Bangalore.

Every Bangalore stall follows roughly the same sequence, whichever vertical or venue you're building for. The exact scope changes with your industry and format, but starting the conversation early gives us more room to get the design right and the compliance paperwork moving well ahead of your show dates.

01

Confirm Venue & Hall Details

BIEC or KTPO, hall number and stall dimensions, plus whether you're in-line, corner or island — this alone decides whether modular or custom makes more sense.

02

Choose Modular Or Custom

Modular suits KTPO and repeatable formats where speed matters; custom and double-decker builds suit BIEC's large-format shows where a distinctive structure is the goal.

03

Send Brand & Product Details

Logo files, brand colours, and what's actually being displayed or demoed — screens, physical products or equipment — so the layout is built around what visitors will see.

04

Flag Compliance Needs Early

Anything unusual — double-decker structure, rigging, heavy equipment — should be flagged as soon as possible so BIEC's structural and electrical approvals don't become the bottleneck.

FAQ

Bangalore exhibition stalls, answered.

Which Bangalore venue is right for my show — BIEC or KTPO?

It depends on your event. Large-format international shows with island or corner positions are usually at BIEC, where double-decker stands and structural compliance approvals are part of the build. Mid-size corporate exhibitions and tech-industry conferences are more often at KTPO Whitefield, where modular and mid-size custom stalls are the norm. Tell us your venue and hall position and we'll size the build accordingly.

How much does an exhibition stall cost in Bangalore?

Cost depends on stall size, format (modular vs. custom), finish level and venue — a modular KTPO stand and a large-format BIEC double-decker sit at very different price points. Share your hall size and requirements and Hayat Stage will send an itemised quote.

How far ahead should I book a stall in Bangalore?

As soon as your hall allocation is confirmed, especially for BIEC — custom and double-decker builds carry longer design, fabrication and compliance-approval timelines than a modular KTPO stand, which generally allows a shorter runway.

Do you build modular stalls, custom stalls, or both?

Both. Modular stalls suit KTPO and repeatable formats where speed and reusability matter; fully custom and double-decker builds suit BIEC's large-format shows where a distinctive, brand-specific structure is the goal. We'll recommend a format based on your venue, budget and timeline.

Which industries do you build exhibition stalls for in Bangalore?

Five verticals: Technology & SaaS, Healthcare & MedTech, Energy & Industrial Manufacturing, Consumer/Retail & Travel, and Corporate Events & Conferences. Bangalore's exhibitor base leans tech, electronics, aerospace and startups, but we build across all five — see the Industries We Build For section above for what each means locally.

Do you handle installation and dismantling at BIEC and KTPO?

Yes — a local install crew handles setup, live support during the show and full dismantling and hall handover at both BIEC and KTPO Whitefield, following each venue's specific access and compliance procedures.

Can you build stages and branded environments beyond the exhibition floor?

Yes — Bangalore's dense concentration of tech campuses and global capability centres means a regular calendar of partner kickoffs, developer events and internal conferences. The same design and fabrication team that builds our exhibition stalls also builds stages, LED backdrops and branded environments for these events.
