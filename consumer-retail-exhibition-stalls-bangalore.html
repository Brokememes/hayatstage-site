Built For Consumer & Retail

Exhibition stalls engineered for Consumer & Retail exhibitors.

Consumer, retail and travel brands need a stall that performs under heavy foot traffic and photographs well from every angle. Hayat Stage builds retail-grade merchandising into the structure — racks, product walls and reception counters — finished to a standard that holds up to a full day of visitors.

At BIEC, weekend consumer and lifestyle expos routinely pull thousands of visitors through the halls once doors open — foot traffic that moves differently from a slower B2B trade show. Aisles fill up fast, browsing patterns get erratic, and a stall has to hold its shape and its finish through it. That means flooring rated for continuous footfall, structural joints that don't work loose over a two- or three-day run, and sightlines planned so visitors can spot your product wall from the aisle without crowding the entrance.

Format depends on where you're showing. A single-product boutique brand at KTPO or a corporate consumer activation in Whitefield usually works well as a modular in-line or corner unit — fast to fabricate, easy to reuse across shows. A travel brand or retail chain anchoring a bigger presence at BIEC often moves into custom or double-decker territory, with an upper deck for hospitality or private meetings and hall compliance sign-off factored into the build from day one.

Merchandising is where retail stalls live or die. We build in the racking, shelving depth and product-wall spacing your actual SKU range needs — not a generic slatwall bolted on afterward — plus a checkout or POS counter positioned so it doesn't choke the aisle, and back-of-house space for stock, packaging and staff breaks that stays out of visitor sightlines.

Retail and travel stands live a second life on Instagram and LinkedIn before the show even ends. We treat lighting temperature, backdrop walls and standee placement as part of the structural brief, not an afterthought — so the booth photographs consistently with your brand colours under BIEC's mixed hall lighting, from the entrance shot down to a close-up of the product wall.

BIEC shows typically run multiple days with a tight move-in and move-out window either side. Finishes need to survive continuous handling — visitors leaning on counters, kids pulling at product displays, staff restocking mid-show — without chipping or looking tired by day two. We fabricate to that standard by default, whether it's a large-format BIEC build or a faster modular turnaround for KTPO.

Planning Ahead

Planning your Consumer & Retail stall in Bangalore.

A custom or double-decker stall at BIEC needs more design and fabrication time than a modular unit reused across KTPO or Whitefield shows — the earlier you bring us in after your hall allocation is confirmed, the more room we have to get structural drawings approved and materials ordered without a last-minute scramble.

01

Confirm Your Footprint

Hall number, stall dimensions and whether you're in-line, corner or island — this decides whether modular or custom makes sense.

02

Share Your Product Range

A rough SKU list or product categories so we can size racking, shelving and product-wall spacing correctly from the first concept.

03

Send Brand Assets

Logo files, brand colours and any existing show photography so the stall photographs consistently with your other marketing.

04

Flag BIEC Compliance Needs

If you're building large-format or double-decker at BIEC, we handle the structural drawings and hall compliance sign-off as part of the build.

BIEC's larger halls are where most large-format and double-decker Consumer & Retail builds happen — anything past a certain footprint or height typically needs structural drawings submitted for hall compliance before fabrication starts, and we build that approval step into the schedule rather than treating it as a surprise. KTPO and Whitefield venues tend to run smaller, faster-turnaround shows where a well-built modular unit gets you a strong stand without the lead time a full custom build demands.

FAQ

Consumer & Retail exhibition stalls in Bangalore, answered.

Can you build large-format or double-decker stalls for BIEC?

Yes — we fabricate to BIEC's hall compliance and structural sign-off requirements for both large-format custom stalls and double-decker builds.

What's the difference between a KTPO stall and a BIEC stall?

KTPO and Whitefield shows tend to run smaller and faster, so a modular or mid-size custom unit usually fits well. BIEC's bigger halls are where large-format and double-decker builds make sense — the format follows the venue and your footprint.

Do you build in product merchandising like racks and shelving?

Yes — retail display fixtures, shelving depth and product-wall spacing are designed into the stall from the concept stage, matched to your actual product range.

Can you fit a checkout or POS counter into the layout?

Yes — we plan point-of-sale counters and back-of-house stock space into the layout so transactions don't crowd the visitor aisle.

How much does a Consumer & Retail stall cost in Bangalore?

It depends on size, format and finish — a KTPO modular unit and a BIEC double-decker sit at very different budgets. Share your hall size and format and we'll send an itemised quote.

Which Bangalore venues do you cover?

Any Bangalore venue, including BIEC, KTPO and Whitefield — plus corporate parks and hotel venues for smaller retail activations.
