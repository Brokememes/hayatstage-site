Built For Technology & SaaS

Exhibition stalls engineered for Technology & SaaS exhibitors.

Technology and software exhibitors live or die on the demo. Hayat Stage builds booths designed around working screens, live product walkthroughs and clean, brand-accurate signage — plus the stages behind partner kickoffs, developer conferences and operations meetings.

What the demo actually needs

Chennai's tech exhibitor base leans more hardware than most — electronics, test equipment and industrial-adjacent technology brands showing physical product alongside software, which changes what the demo needs from the stall. A live walkthrough still needs planned power and sightlines to the demo station, but a hardware or test-equipment demo also needs a counter or bay built to actually hold the unit — enough structural depth and load capacity that the product looks displayed, not perched. We plan for both from the concept stage, whether the stand is running a software dashboard or a piece of test instrumentation.

Who this vertical covers

"Technology" in Chennai stretches from SaaS platforms to RF and microwave test-equipment brands and antenna and test-systems specialists showing instrumentation on the floor — we've built demo bays for exactly that kind of equipment stand. It also covers security and industrial-technology brands whose stalls need to double as a manufacturing-capability showcase, tying into Chennai's identity as one of India's electronics and auto-component manufacturing hubs.

Booths — and the stages behind them

Alongside CTC exhibition stalls, we build the stages behind partner events and operations meetings for tech and electronics companies with a Chennai or South India base — the OMR IT corridor in particular has a dense concentration of product engineering and GCC teams who run internal and partner events off the trade-show calendar. Same crew, same design discipline, whether it's a stall on the CTC floor or a stage inside a Chennai hotel.

Chennai Trade Centre in Nandambakkam is effectively the city's one major exhibition venue for a technology or electronics show, which means custom, modular and double-decker formats all get fabricated to the same set of CTC compliance and layout requirements rather than juggling multiple venue rulebooks. That's an advantage for planning — once we know your hall and stall number, the compliance path is settled early.

Planning Ahead

Planning Your Tech Stall In Chennai.

Chennai's tech exhibitors more often need to plan around physical product — test equipment, hardware units, sometimes heavier instrumentation — alongside the usual screens and signage. That changes what's worth sorting out before the first call.

Lead Time

CTC custom and double-decker stalls need fabrication lead time similar to any major trade-show build — start as soon as your hall allocation is confirmed, and flag early if any demo hardware is being shipped in or needs its own installation window.

What To Bring To The First Call

Your brand guidelines, a list of what's being demoed including any physical hardware or test units and their weight, power and space needs, and your CTC hall size and stall position.

Chennai Logistics

CTC Nandambakkam is the default venue for nearly every technology show in the city, so we build to its compliance and layout requirements as a matter of course. If your team is based in the OMR corridor, we can also coordinate a stage or event build closer to your own campus.

FAQ

Technology & SaaS exhibition stalls in Chennai, answered.

Can you integrate live product demos into a tech booth?

Yes — we plan power, networking and sightlines into the layout from the start, and for hardware or test-equipment exhibitors, we build demo bays with the structural depth to hold the unit properly, not just a laptop stand.

Do you build stages for partner kickoffs and operations meetings, not just booths?

Yes — LED backdrops, stage sets and branded environments for partner kickoffs and ops meetings are part of our regular Chennai tech-vertical scope, alongside CTC exhibition stalls.

How much does a stall cost in Chennai?

It depends on size, format, finish and whether the stand needs to carry hardware or test equipment. Share your hall size and what's being demoed and we'll send an itemised quote.

Which venues do you cover in Chennai?

Any Chennai venue, most commonly Chennai Trade Centre (CTC) in Nandambakkam, which is the city's primary venue for technology and electronics shows.

Can the stall carry heavier test or hardware equipment, not just screens?

Yes — we build demo bays and counters with the structural capacity for real instrumentation and hardware units, which is a regular requirement for Chennai's electronics and test-equipment exhibitors.

How far in advance should we start planning a Chennai stall?

As soon as your CTC hall allocation is confirmed. If any demo hardware is being shipped or imported, tell us early — that timeline often matters more than the stall build itself.
