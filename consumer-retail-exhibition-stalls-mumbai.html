Built For Consumer & Retail

Exhibition stalls engineered for Consumer & Retail exhibitors.

Consumer, retail and travel brands need a stall that performs under heavy foot traffic and photographs well from every angle. Hayat Stage builds retail-grade merchandising into the structure — racks, product walls and reception counters — finished to a standard that holds up to a full day of visitors.

Mumbai splits into two very different show environments, and the build has to match. NESCO's Bombay Exhibition Centre in Goregaon hosts the city's biggest consumer, apparel and lifestyle trade shows — dense halls, heavy walk-through traffic, and a stall that needs to hold its ground for the full run. Jio World Convention Centre at BKC pulls a different crowd: corporate and finance-sector delegates who expect a quieter, more polished stand rather than a loud retail floor presence.

That difference shapes format choice. A travel brand or apparel retailer anchoring a hall at NESCO usually wants a custom or double-decker build — enough footprint for racking, a full product wall and an upper deck for buyer meetings. A finance or premium consumer brand presenting at JWCC is more often better served by a tighter, design-forward footprint where the finish and materials do the talking rather than sheer scale.

Merchandising still matters at both venues, just differently. At NESCO we're usually building out full racking runs, shelving sized to a real SKU count, and a POS or checkout counter positioned so it doesn't block the aisle during peak hours. At JWCC the "merchandising" is often lighter — a curated product wall or a handful of hero pieces — because the goal is a considered brand moment, not a retail counter.

Photography considerations differ too. NESCO's halls run bright and busy, so we lean on strong backdrop walls and consistent brand lighting to help a stand cut through visual noise in photos. JWCC's more controlled, premium environment rewards subtler lighting and cleaner sightlines — the kind of finish that photographs well for a LinkedIn recap rather than a high-traffic reel.

Both venues run multi-day schedules with tight move-in windows, so finishes are specified to survive continuous handling — visitors leaning on counters at NESCO, or a steady flow of delegates and press at JWCC — without chipping, scuffing or looking tired by the final day.

Planning Ahead

Planning your Consumer & Retail stall in Mumbai.

The first decision is really "which Mumbai." A NESCO hall presence and a JWCC corporate stand are different projects with different design language, different lead times and different levels of merchandising — telling us which venue and which audience you're building for lets us scope the right format from the first call.

01

Tell Us Your Venue

NESCO Goregaon or JWCC BKC — the venue and audience shape whether this is a retail-floor build or a premium corporate stand.

02

Share Your Product Range

For NESCO retail builds, a SKU list or category breakdown so we can size racking and product-wall spacing correctly.

03

Send Brand Assets

Logo files, brand colours and reference imagery so the finish matches the register your audience expects at that venue.

04

Confirm Footprint & Format

Hall position and stall dimensions decide whether a custom double-decker or a tighter design-forward build makes sense.

NESCO's larger halls host most of the large-format and double-decker Consumer & Retail work we do in Mumbai — a bigger footprint means more structural planning and a longer fabrication runway, so we get moving on drawings as soon as your hall allocation is confirmed. JWCC at BKC generally works with tighter footprints and a more design-led brief, which can move faster once the concept is locked, but its venue standards call for a cleaner, more restrained build than a typical trade-show floor.

FAQ

Consumer & Retail exhibition stalls in Mumbai, answered.

Do you build large-format or double-decker stalls for NESCO?

Yes — NESCO's Bombay Exhibition Centre in Goregaon is where most of our large-format and double-decker Consumer & Retail builds for Mumbai happen, sized for its major trade-show halls.

Is a Jio World Convention Centre stall different from a NESCO stall?

Usually, yes. JWCC at BKC tends to host corporate and finance-sector audiences, so those stands lean toward a premium, understated finish. NESCO shows skew toward higher-volume consumer footfall, so those builds are engineered more heavily around durability and merchandising.

Do you build in product merchandising like racks and shelving?

Yes — retail display fixtures, shelving depth and product-wall spacing are designed into the stall from the concept stage, matched to your actual product range.

Can you fit a checkout or POS counter into the layout?

Yes — we plan point-of-sale counters and back-of-house stock space into the layout so transactions don't crowd the visitor aisle.

How much does a Consumer & Retail stall cost in Mumbai?

It depends heavily on venue and format — a NESCO large-format build and a premium JWCC stand sit at very different budgets. Share your hall size and format and we'll send an itemised quote.

Which Mumbai venues do you cover?

Any Mumbai venue, including NESCO Goregaon and Jio World Convention Centre at BKC.
