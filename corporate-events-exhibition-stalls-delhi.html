Built For Corporate Events

Exhibition stalls engineered for Corporate Events exhibitors.

Foundation days, award ceremonies and company-wide kickoffs need a different kind of build than a trade-show booth — large-format entrance structures, stages rated for thousands of attendees, and lighting and AV that carry a room. This is where Hayat Stage's event-production side takes over from stall fabrication.

Full AV & Lighting Production

Sound, lighting and LED walls run as one production unit, not separately contracted vendors.

A booth is built to be walked past; an entrance structure for a foundation day or a national-scale conference is built to be walked through, by a crowd that can run into the thousands — which changes the engineering, not just the branding. We plan entrance arches and signage walls for footfall and wind load, route cabling so red carpets stay trip-free, and on stage, move from a booth counter's light load to a deck rated for presenters, trophy tables and often a performance, with overhead truss carrying moving-head lighting, line-array sound and an LED wall rigged to spec. At a venue like Pragati Maidan or Bharat Mandapam (IICC), that also means designing to the centre's structural and protocol requirements from the outset — IICC events often carry a level of security and VIP-movement planning that a standard trade-show booth never has to account for.

This side of our work covers a wide range of formats beyond a single conference booth. We've built entrance arches and red-carpet experiences for foundation-day anniversaries, staged award ceremonies with trophy platforms and cued lighting, produced stages for town halls and company-wide kickoffs, and built photo backdrop walls and interview sets for product launches and press events. Delhi's calendar includes a heavier share of national-scale conferences and flagship industry events than most other cities we cover, given IICC's role as a national exhibition venue — which tends to raise the bar on protocol, signage clearances and stage scale.

A stall is usually one contractor's scope. A corporate stage at the scale Delhi events often run at pulls in a carpenter, a rigger, a lighting designer, a sound engineer and frequently a livestream or broadcast operator — coordinating that many vendors into a single load-in window, on top of a venue's own protocol requirements, is a lot to manage from your side. We run stage, AV and lighting as one production unit: one site walkthrough that accounts for IICC's structural and protocol sign-off, one rigging and cabling plan, one crew on the ground, and one point of contact if the schedule shifts. That single-team accountability tends to matter most exactly where Delhi events get complicated — high-profile guest lists, tight security windows and a flagship venue's own approval process.

Planning Ahead

Planning a Corporate Event in Delhi.

Before you reach out, it helps to have a sense of three things: roughly how many people are attending, the event format (an award ceremony, a national conference, a town hall), and a target date. If Pragati Maidan or Bharat Mandapam (IICC) is even a possibility, say so early — the venue's structural and protocol requirements shape the entrance and stage design from the concept stage, and events there often involve VIP movement or security planning that needs to be built into the layout, not added afterward.

Lead time matters most for large-format entrance structures and multi-truss stage rigs, which need structural drawings signed off before load-in, and IICC's calendar as a flagship national venue tends to be in high demand — so the earlier your date and venue are confirmed, the more design and approval time we have to work with.

Share your attendee count, format and venue and we'll put together a first concept and quote.
