Built For Energy & Industrial

Exhibition stalls engineered for Energy & Industrial exhibitors.

Energy and industrial exhibitors bring genuinely heavy product to the show floor. Hayat Stage builds stalls with the load-bearing structure and floor rating to carry real machinery and components, finished with the sustainability-forward materials this sector increasingly expects.

Pragati Maidan and Bharat Mandapam host India's largest national and international industrial and energy trade fairs, often with government ministries, PSUs and national pavilions sharing floor space with private OEMs. That means stall floors here regularly carry full-scale machinery, turbine or grid-equipment mock-ups and multi-tonne exhibits at a scale few other Indian venues see routinely — so the flooring and sub-frame engineering isn't a nice-to-have, it's what determines whether the exhibit can go up at all.

Sustainability-forward material choices do double duty at a national-showcase venue like Bharat Mandapam. For clean-energy exhibitors, natural finishes, living elements and reusable structure aren't just a brand preference — they're part of how the stand reads to a very wide, often policy-facing audience watching India's energy transition play out on a national stage. That raises the bar on execution: materials have to look considered next to a government pavilion, not just next to a competitor's booth.

IICC's structural and protocol requirements go beyond load-bearing sign-off. Pavilion-format and government-adjacent halls at Bharat Mandapam carry protocol considerations — VIP movement, security clearance patterns — that affect stall layout, sightlines and access points in ways a purely commercial hall wouldn't. Building for Delhi means designing around those constraints from the start, not adjusting after the fact.

Pragati Maidan and Bharat Mandapam support custom, modular, double-decker and pavilion-format builds, all fabricated to IICC's structural and protocol requirements. Pavilion format specifically suits energy and industrial exhibitors taking part in a national or ministry-led group presentation rather than a standalone commercial booth, while OEMs and EPC companies more often build custom or double-decker stands for a flagship national fair appearance.

FAQ

Energy & Industrial exhibition stalls in Delhi, answered.

Can you handle IICC's structural and protocol sign-off for a Bharat Mandapam build?

Yes — Pragati Maidan and Bharat Mandapam builds go through IICC's own structural and protocol requirements, and we prepare the documentation and layout for that process as part of the build.

Do you build pavilion-format stalls for national or ministry-led group presentations?

Yes — Bharat Mandapam supports pavilion-format builds for exhibitors taking part in a national or ministry-led group presentation, alongside standalone custom and double-decker stalls.

Can your stalls carry full-scale machinery or grid-equipment displays?

Yes — Pragati Maidan regularly hosts multi-tonne exhibits, and we engineer flooring and framing for full-scale machinery, turbine mock-ups and grid-equipment displays.

What's the lead time for a double-decker or pavilion build at Bharat Mandapam?

Longer than a standard modular stand — IICC's structural and protocol approvals take real time, so we recommend starting the conversation as soon as your hall is booked.

Do you build sustainability-led stands for clean-energy exhibitors at a national venue?

Yes — natural finishes, living elements and reusable structure work well here, especially for exhibitors positioning themselves in India's clean-energy transition to a wide audience.

Which formats do you build at Pragati Maidan and Bharat Mandapam?

Custom, modular, double-decker and pavilion-format stalls, all fabricated to IICC's structural and protocol requirements.
